## Outreach draft A: short and personal (~120 words)

Subject: 15 minutes on a tool for tracking IRCC changes against your caseload?

Hi [Name],

I'm Chi. I moved to Canada from Nigeria 5 years ago and I live here in Ottawa. I'm building something called Argus, and I'd love your read on it.

Argus is a multi-agent tool that watches IRCC policy changes and cross-references them against the caseload you've saved. When something shifts (like the March 25 2025 CRS update that removed job-offer points), it flags every affected client and drafts action recommendations.

I'm not selling. I'm collecting 15-min feedback calls from RCICs I respect, and your name kept coming up when I looked at solo and small Ottawa firms running real Express Entry volume.

Do you have 15 minutes in the next two weeks? Any time that works for you.

Chi

## Outreach draft B: longer and professional (~200 words)

Hello,

My name is Chi. I'm an Ottawa-based founder (Nigerian, arrived 2020) building a tool for Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultants, and I'm looking for 15 minutes of your team's time. I'm not selling anything and there's no product to buy yet.

The tool is called Argus. It's a multi-agent system that continuously watches IRCC (policy pages, program updates, Ministerial Instructions, CRS scorecard shifts) and cross-references every change against the caseload a consultant has saved. When something moves, like the March 25 2025 CRS update that stripped job-offer points, Argus surfaces the exact clients affected and drafts a first-pass action recommendation for the consultant to review, edit, or reject.

I chose your firm because you run real Ottawa caseload and your practice has the shape I want to design for: a small, senior team where a missed IRCC change costs real hours, not a huge firm with a compliance department. I want to understand what you actually do today when IRCC changes something, what breaks, and whether Argus would help or get in the way.

A 15-min call is all I'm asking for. I'll bring a two-slide demo and a lot of questions.

Chi

## Notes on the register and methodology

The official CICC register lives at register.college-ic.ca/Public-Register-EN/Public-Register-EN/RCIC_Search.aspx. It's an ASP.NET postback form that resisted programmatic search this session (browser tab reset after the search postback fired). To stay honest we pulled the CICC-fed public mirror at RateMyRCIC (ratemyrcic.ca/consultants/ottawa, 128 Ottawa RCICs across 6 pages, active/inactive flagged) and confirmed each name plus R# against the consultant's own firm website where one exists. In-house RCICs at uOttawa, Carleton, and IDRC were skipped since they don't take private clients. Emails on the shortlist come from firm websites, not the register. Phones on RateMyRCIC are partially masked.
