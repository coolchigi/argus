# Outreach drafts

Six ready-to-send emails. Copy and paste. Voice checked against `~/.claude/voice-dna.md`.

Argus positioning is honest but light on jargon here. RCICs are not LLM engineers. The pitch is: **"I built a tool that watches IRCC policy changes and flags which of your clients are affected. Would you look at a 5-minute demo?"**

The winning phrase across all five cold emails is the March 25, 2025 CRS scorecard change that removed job-offer points. It's a real wound your recipients felt. Lead with it.

## Cadence

- Send all 6 the same day, morning if possible.
- Track responses in a simple sheet or in `research/outreach-log.md` (create when the first reply lands).
- Follow up on non-responses at Day 7 with a two-line bump.
- Don't send more than 2 follow-ups. Silence at 14 days is a "no" for our purposes. Move on.

## Personalisation reminders

- If Elsie or Emine reply, be ready to reference a specific post they made. Skim their most recent 3 posts before the call.
- If Mario replies, offer to run the call in French if he prefers.
- If Tarek replies, be ready to talk PMP briefly (he'll like the "structured workflow diff" framing).

## What NOT to say in the demo

- No LLM jargon. Say "AI reads the IRCC page" not "Bedrock Nova Pro inference."
- No "multi-agent." Say "multiple checks" or "adversarial review" only if they ask about accuracy.
- No competitor bashing. Never say "unlike Officio" out loud. Let them bring up incumbents themselves.
- No pricing on the first call. Say "still figuring out pricing, want to see if the shape is useful first."
