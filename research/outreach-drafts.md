**Attach to every cold email:** `research/ircc-digest-2025-09.pdf` (3 pages, 13 KB). This is the value prop. Do not send the cold emails without it.

Voice checked against `~/.claude/voice-dna.md`. No em dashes, no semicolons, no negative parallelisms.

## Cadence

- Send all 6 on the same morning, ideally Tue-Thu 8-10am Ottawa time.
- Track responses in `research/outreach-log.md` (create when the first reply lands).
- Day 7 bump on non-responses: two lines, no re-pitch, just "adding you to the list, see attached update" with the same PDF.
- Two bumps maximum. Silence at day 14 is a no. Move on.

## Personalisation reminders

- Skim each recipient's most recent 3 posts before their reply call. Reference something specific.
- If Mario replies, run the call in French if he prefers.
- If Tarek replies, PMP framing ("structured workflow diff") lands well.
- If Elsie replies, offer to be a source for one of her videos on Argus once the demo is live.

## What NOT to say

- No LLM jargon in the emails or on the call. Say "AI reads IRCC pages," not "Bedrock Nova Pro inference."
- No "multi-agent." "Multiple checks" or "adversarial review" only if they ask about accuracy.
- No competitor bashing. Never say "unlike Officio" out loud. Let them raise incumbents.
- No pricing on the first call. Say "still figuring out pricing, want to see if the shape is useful first."
